Plan the full cost of getting ready to open

A fit-out quotation is only one part of the commitment. Separate the project budget from the money needed to operate the business.

Build a complete project budget

Allow for surveys, design and specialist inputs, applicable project charges, demolition, civil work, services, interiors, signage, furniture, equipment and installation. Clarify taxes, delivery and commissioning. Identify landlord deposits or fit-out charges separately. Track business costs such as rent during works, staff setup, opening stock and working capital outside the construction total so they are not overlooked.

Keep uncertainty visible

An early estimate is based on assumptions. Refine it as measurements, equipment schedules, specifications and quotations become available. Keep a project-specific contingency for uncertainty and track optional upgrades separately. Existing buildings may reveal concealed conditions after work begins.

Compare like with like

Check area definitions, quantities, material standards, equipment specifications and exclusions. A price per square foot is not a complete comparison. Confirm whether service upgrades, landlord interfaces, disposal, access equipment and after-hours work are included where relevant.

Common mistakes to avoid

Signing before checking suitability

A better decision
Review the use, layout, services and landlord conditions early.

Maximising seats or desks first

A better decision
Test circulation, support spaces and the operating model together.

Ordering equipment too late

A better decision
Confirm dimensions, loads and connections before detailed coordination.

Spending on visible finishes first

A better decision
Resolve essential infrastructure and operational needs before upgrades.

Approving verbal changes

A better decision
Record cost, timing and effects on other trades before authorising.

Rushing to open

A better decision
Allow time for testing, snags, required authorisations and staff setup.

Agree fees and contractual terms with the appointed team. No fixed fee percentage, saving or completion guarantee applies to every commercial project.
