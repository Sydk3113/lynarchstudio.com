01 / At home

Residential Spaces

Homes, apartments and renovations. Start with everyday life, then work through the team, budget and build.

Watch the residential spaces guide 1 min 15 sec

Residential spaces · 1 min 15 sec

Before you build a home.

What to decide before choosing finishes, from how you move through a room to how you plan the budget and prepare for handover.

Read the transcript

Planning a home? Before choosing finishes, stop and think about how you want the space to work. It's vital to start with your lifestyle, not just the look.

An architect connects your needs, your site and your budget, helping you make important decisions, while they are still much easier to change during the early design phase.

True design means looking beyond appearance. We consider daylight, shade, privacy, storage, and the everyday movement of people through rooms to create a home that feels naturally comfortable.

The process begins with a clear brief, develops into coordinated drawings and moves towards contractor selection and construction with responsibilities agreed upon clearly at every single project stage.

Architects, interior designers and contractors bring different skills. Clear coordination is the absolute secret to helping the design and the physical work on site come together seamlessly for you.

Set a realistic budget, allow for uncertainty, and review changes before committing. Good design supports informed choices, helping you navigate the complexities of building a beautiful new space.

At handover, check the details, record outstanding work, and take the time to understand how to care for your space. It ensures your long-term investment is properly protected.

02 / At work

Commercial Spaces

Offices, shops, cafés and restaurants. From checking the premises to planning the fit-out and opening day.

Watch the commercial spaces guide 1 min 1 sec

Commercial spaces · 1 min 1 sec

From fit-out to opening day.

See what to check before you commit to a space and what to plan before you open the doors.

Read the transcript

Planning a commercial space? Stop and think. Before you decide how it should look, start with how your business actually works. That is the true foundation of every great design.

Every business has unique needs. An office requires space for deep focus and collaboration. A shop needs clear browsing paths. A cafe needs a practical, efficient flow for your customers.

A design team connects those specific needs with the physical space, budget and technical systems. We help important decisions happen early before the construction work even begins on your project.

Before committing to any premises, check the layout, available services and landlord conditions. Confirm exactly which specialist reviews and permissions your project needs. It saves you time and major headaches.

During the fit-out, keep responsibilities and changes clear. Before opening, allow sufficient time for testing, snagging, and final handover, as well as crucial business preparations to ensure you are ready.
